Questions

Before you call

How fast can a plumber get to me?

Emergency calls are dispatched right away, and you hear a specific arrival window when you call. Non-urgent jobs can usually be booked the same or next day.

Will I know the price before the work starts?

Yes. The plumber diagnoses the problem, gives you a price, and starts only after you say yes.

What should I do while I wait for the plumber?

For a burst pipe or active leak, shut off the main water valve, usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Turn off the water heater if it is affected and move valuables away from the water.

Do you work on weekends and holidays?

Emergency plumbing is available 24 hours a day, every day of the week, including holidays.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Major credit cards, debit cards, and checks. Ask about financing for larger jobs like water heater replacement or repiping.
